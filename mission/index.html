Our Mission · Starting in Rwanda

AI opportunity should not depend on where you grow up.

Bringing AI education and tools to students in the Global South, starting in Rwanda.

Why we exist

Access is only the beginning.

AI tools are becoming part of how people learn, work, and create. But access to a tool does not automatically teach someone how to use it well, question its answers, or make something meaningful with it.

We want young people to develop curiosity, independent thinking, and practical AI skills—not just learn to copy a prompt.

What we're building

Learning by doing.

Boundlessy's Wonder Room is an interactive learning space. Students practice asking clearer questions in Prompt Quest and evaluating evidence in AI Detective.

Our goal is to help learners become confident, thoughtful users and creators of AI, while testing and improving the experience with real feedback.

Our starting point

Built with students in mind.

We're preparing a school-based pilot in Kigali for approximately 45 secondary-school students. The early experience is browser-based, designed for ordinary school computers, and focused on skills that can be practiced immediately.

We're treating this as a learning pilot—not claiming proven educational impact before we have evidence. We want to observe where students get stuck, what they enjoy, and whether their reasoning improves through practice.

The bigger ambition

From one classroom to more possibilities.

Starting in Rwanda, we hope to make practical, playful AI education more accessible to students across the Global South. We believe students should be able to ask ambitious questions, test ideas, and participate in shaping an AI-enabled future.

Schools, educators, researchers, and technology partners can help by sharing feedback, supporting access to learning tools and equipment, or contributing their expertise.
