WHY RWANDA?

Start small. Learn fast. Scale what works.

Rwanda is our test bed. Inspired by the way Zipline pioneered and refined its delivery model here before expanding, we'll work directly with students, test what helps them learn, and improve through real feedback.

Then we'll bring what works online, making the experience available to students globally.
