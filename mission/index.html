BOUNDLESSY · OUR MISSION

Advancing AI diffusion through education.

Bringing AI education and tools to students across the Global South, starting in Rwanda.

WHAT

Discover. Question. Create.

We build playful, hands-on AI learning experiences where students explore real tools, challenge answers, and make things of their own.

WHY

From using AI to shaping it.

The next generation should do more than consume technology. They should have the confidence to experiment, think independently, and create with it.

HOW

Start in a classroom. Think beyond borders.

We begin with students in Rwanda, using existing school computer labs and learning through experimentation. What works can grow across the Global South.
