LEARNING BY DOING

Real tools. Real ideas. Real projects.

Every Saturday, students explore AI through hands-on sessions in their school computer lab. They learn how to ask better questions, experiment with new tools, and turn their own ideas into practical projects—whether for school, everyday life, or their communities.

FROM PILOT TO POSSIBILITY

Start with one school.
Build a model others can use.

The Rwanda pilot is where we test the curriculum, learn from students and teachers, and document what works. The ambition is a repeatable, locally adaptable learning experience for schools across the Global South.

01

Prove the experience

Work with 45 Senior 5 students at one participating school with 30 computers available.

02

Show the creations

Capture projects, student reflections, and learning progress—with consent and care.

03

Share what works

Refine a practical program that can be adapted by other schools and communities.

Expansion is the vision, not a claimed outcome of the pilot.

Our measure of success

From access to ability.
From ability to opportunity.

We will track what students learn, build and continue doing. We won't confuse ambition with achieved impact.

↗ WHY I STARTED

You don't need to know how to code to start building.

For years, not knowing how to code felt like a limit on what I could create. That moment made me think: maybe this is the era when I stop letting that limit define what is possible. And how much more exciting would it be to learn alongside students, so that 20 years from now, they won't have to wonder why nobody showed them the world of possibilities opening up around them.

Fionah Umulisa

Founder, GrearX
